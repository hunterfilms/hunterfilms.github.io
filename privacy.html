Privacy Policy: Hunter Films Editor

Hunter Films Editor is a private tool operated by Hunter Films LLC for its own use. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses: with the account owner's permission, it reads video files from, and saves finished videos to, Google Drive folders belonging to Hunter Films LLC.

How data is used: only to edit and deliver those videos. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage: files are processed temporarily and are not kept outside Google Drive after editing.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
